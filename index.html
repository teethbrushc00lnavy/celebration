Getting there

The venue

Anantara Lawana Koh Samui Resort

Open in Maps

On the northern, quieter end of Chaweng Beach, only a few minutes' drive from the airport.

By air

Fly into Samui Airport (USM), about 5 to 10 minutes from the resort. Most routes from Europe connect through Bangkok or Singapore, so plan for at least one stop. Yes, it's a trek. Yes, it's worth it.

The resort can arrange airport transfers for a fee. We'll share group transfer details closer to the date.

The boring but important bit

As of 15 September 2026, passports from Germany, Austria, the UK and Singapore can enter Thailand without a visa for tourism for up to 30 days. Your passport should be valid for at least six months. Thailand may also ask for an online digital arrival card before you land. Rules change, so please check the Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs or your own government's travel advice for your passport.

Good to know

What should I wear?

White. All of it. It's warm and humid in late September with the odd shower, so think linen and cotton, and pack a spare outfit. Yes, white shows everything. That's your problem.

What's the weather like?

Around 29°C and humid, with showers likely, so bring something for the rain. The sea is warm, and the resort has a beachfront infinity pool. Your hair will frizz. We've accepted this.

Can I bring a plus-one? Or the kids?

Add everyone who'd like to come as a separate guest in the RSVP and we'll confirm. Surprise guests are for birthdays. Confirm who's invited, including kids. The resort has a kids' club.
